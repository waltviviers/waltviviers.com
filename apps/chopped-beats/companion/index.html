Chopped Beats is made for a computer. Use your phone to record your voice or grab sounds from videos, then send them over and make the song on your PC.

    Getting sounds onto your computer
    1. Easiest: keep your Chopped Beats folder inside Google Drive or OneDrive on your PC. Tap Send, choose Drive or OneDrive, and save into the folder's samples folder. The sound shows up in Chopped Beats by itself within a few seconds.
    2. Or: tap Send and send it to yourself (WhatsApp, email, Nearby Share, AirDrop). On your PC, drop the file onto Chopped Beats.

    Nothing is uploaded to this site. Sounds go only where you send them.
