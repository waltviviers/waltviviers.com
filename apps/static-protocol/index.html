How To Play

This is the runner's phone. Five jobs come through tonight. Tap a notification to open the app it lands in, then pick how you handle it.

Every choice triggers a live SYNC — a short minigame, different for every job. Tap the prompts, or use arrow keys / WASD / Space on desktop. Clean it and your choice plays out exactly as intended; fumble it and the job gets forced sideways.

Fumble too many and the heat — the signal bars, top right — climbs. Three strikes and the job ends for you, whatever you decide next.

Ten different endings are waiting, built from who you trusted, how you got it done, and how steady your hands were.
