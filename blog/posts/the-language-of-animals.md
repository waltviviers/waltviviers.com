I enjoy the language of animals, it's not spoken with the tongue, but rather expressed with the whole body. It is honest and primal, no room for sales pitches, no time for b.s. The eyes, the skin, and the muscles, the way they move: flowing with information. The human animal too. A smooth hand lifts no bricks, tired eyes cannot pretend to smile. Actions speak louder than words, and inaction reveals true nature. I enjoy the language of animals because for all the creative sounds we manipulate each other with, inside we are undeniably primal.
