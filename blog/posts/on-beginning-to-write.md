title: On Beginning to Write
date: 2026-09-03
description: A short note on why I'm starting to keep a written journal alongside the paintings.
---

For years I've said what I had to say in paint. A canvas doesn't argue back; it just asks you to keep looking. But lately I've felt the pull to set some of it down in words too — the thinking that happens *before* the brush, and the quieter things that happen after.

So this is a beginning. Not a manifesto, and not a schedule I intend to keep religiously. Just a place to write when a piece of work, a book, or an ordinary afternoon in the studio leaves me with something worth turning over.

## What you'll find here

Essays, mostly. Some will be about the practical grain of the work — a medium that misbehaved, a commission that taught me something, the slow business of making a living as an artist in Pretoria. Others will wander further afield.

> A painting is finished when it stops asking questions. An essay, I'm learning, is finished when you run out of honest ones.

If you'd like to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/feed.xml) — point your reader at it and new pieces will arrive on their own. No newsletter, no account, no algorithm deciding what you see.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
