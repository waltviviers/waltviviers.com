I plant a word seed,\
and out grows some poet tree,\
I play a word game,\
with this poetry within me.

In this tree is an industry:\
words hanging like sweet fruit,\
rolling around in my mouth,\
a flavourful rainbow,\
ice cream candy explosion.

I devour the fruit,\
but the eating is moot,\
for the fruit from the poet tree\
grows from within me.
