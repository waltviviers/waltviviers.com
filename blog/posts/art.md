I've always been interested in art, I'm particularly drawn to Chiaroscuro in painting and Comics Art in drawing. I've spent a lot of time with traditional materials, but these days I work mostly in inks (linework) and digitally (colour). Professionally I've worked my way up from an Apprentice in a Tattoo Parlour to Creative Director in a niche advertising agency.
