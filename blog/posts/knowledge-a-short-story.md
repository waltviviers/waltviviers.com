SciFi idea: in the future, all knowledge is downloaded to the brain (Matrix style) the catch? People are divided into social classes based on the bandwidth speed the can afford (or not afford). The twist? A natural genius wins the day by devising a new hypothesis, theories and facts. The lesson? When knowledge is downloaded, everyone thinks the same.
