About This Form

This is the official delivery and consignment note for Walt Viviers Fine Art. It is completed at the time of delivery and records each artwork left with a gallery or representative on consignment.

The form captures the date of delivery, the name of the person receiving the work, and a line-by-line record of each piece — including its reference code, title and description, dimensions, and retail price in ZAR.

The recipient’s signature confirms the work arrived in perfect condition. Download the PDF to print a blank copy for completion on delivery.

Conditions

Please notify the artist by SMS, e-mail or telephonically upon the sale of artwork.

Payment is to be made at the end of the month in which the artwork is sold, or at the latest by the 25th day of the following month.

If works are returned to the artist, they must be in the same condition as when received by the gallery. The recipient’s signature below confirms that the delivered work (including frames, if any) is undamaged, unscratch, and clean.

If returned works are damaged, the gallery will receive an invoice for repairs at a rate of R200 per hour.
