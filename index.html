A Painter's Eye
Behind the Lens

Photography and video are an extension of the same practice — the same instinct for light, composition, and the quiet moment that says everything. I bring a fine artist's eye to every frame.

I work with people first: portrait sessions, events, and wellness retreats where the aim is to capture something true rather than posed — unhurried, comfortable, and attentive to the story unfolding in front of the camera.

Based in Pretoria and available across South Africa and beyond, I shoot in both photo and video, and every session is delivered as a private online album.

Whether it's a single portrait or a multi-day retreat, tell me what you have in mind and I'll shape the session around it.

People and Art
at the Core

Bio & Statement ↗

My path has always been creative and service-minded — early studies in directing and design, then apprenticeships and freelance work, always focused on making things and serving the people who need them.

I've worked bars and boardrooms, apprenticed as a tattoo artist and body piercer, and built a long career that led to Creative Director — running small businesses on the side and painting, illustrating and photographing the whole way through.

After a lifetime in design and marketing, I made a deliberate choice to step back from managing others and return to making things myself. I'm a creative polymath at heart, and with the rise of AI I think every creative will end up working across disciplines rather than just directing them.

That shift started in lockdown, when remote work let me bring hands-on making back into the mix — painting, photography, writing, video. Now I'm doing it full-time: exploring what AI-assisted tools can add, and following whatever curiosity pulls me next.
