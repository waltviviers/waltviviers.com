How we work

From first call to final report.

A clear process means HR spends less time coordinating and more time seeing results.

  1. Discover

    A short call and survey to understand your people, pressures and goals.

  2. Design

    We propose a programme, calendar and budget that fits your workplace.

  3. Deliver

    Our practitioners run every session on-site or online. You just book the room.

  4. Measure

    Participation, feedback and health trends in a report for leadership.

Who we are

Empowering teams, elevating culture.

Manifesto Wellness Africa is a purpose-driven corporate wellness company. We believe healthy teams build thriving businesses, so we create experiences that inspire, recharge and empower employees, designed around your company’s goals and your team’s needs.

Our mission is to partner with businesses to create workplaces where wellbeing isn’t just a benefit, it’s a way of life.

  • Tailored experiences

    Every programme, workshop and gift is designed around your team’s needs and your company’s objectives.

  • Holistic approach

    We work with mind, body and spirit, so the impact on your people and culture lasts.

  • Proven results

    Higher engagement, lower stress, and happier, more productive teams.
