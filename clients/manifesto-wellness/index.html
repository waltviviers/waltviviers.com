Why it matters
When your people feel well, it shows in the work, the culture and the numbers.
  • Fewer sick days and less presenteeism, when people are at work but running on empty.
  • A visible employee benefit that helps you attract and keep good people.
  • Support that works alongside your medical aid and EAP, not instead of it.
  • One trusted partner instead of five separate suppliers to manage.
How we work

From first call to final report.

A clear process means HR spends less time coordinating and more time seeing results.

  1. Discover

    A short call and survey to understand your people, pressures and goals.

  2. Design

    We propose a programme, calendar and budget that fits your workplace.

  3. Deliver

    Our practitioners run every session on-site or online. You just book the room.

  4. Measure

    Participation, feedback and health trends in a report for leadership.
